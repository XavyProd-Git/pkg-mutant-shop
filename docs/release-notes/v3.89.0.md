## Added

- **Folder apps install.** An app folder such as RetroArch is sent to the drive ShadowMountPlus
  watches and mounts from; a PS4 is told why instead of being sent one it cannot mount.

## Fixed

- **Homebrews install again on both consoles.** They were served under a path containing a space,
  which a console's installer cannot fetch.
- **The PS5 could send a truncated reply** once its answer grew past a fixed size.
- **An app mounted from a drive shows as installed** instead of being listed as missing while it sits
  on the home screen.
