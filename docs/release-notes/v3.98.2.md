## Fixed

- **A button that is looking up a release says so, instead of saying "Working…".** Pressing
  Download or Update on a console spends its first seconds reading what the project has published —
  and on a project the console has not asked about before, that read is a cold fetch. Labelling all
  of it "Working…" is why the wait felt like nothing happening: nothing *was* downloading yet, and
  if the lookup failed the next thing you saw was a message about GitHub. It now reads **"Checking
  the latest release…"** until a transfer actually starts, in all fifteen languages.
- **"could not reach GitHub" is finally translated where it is actually shown.** The sentence was
  given a proper translation in 3.97.2, but two of the places that display it printed the raw
  English anyway because they bypassed the translator. Those were the two that matter — a Download
  and an Update on a console.
- **The PS4 no longer invents seven USB drives.** `/api/devices` reported usb0 through usb6 as
  present, each with 512 KB of space, on a console with nothing plugged in — because the slots
  exist as empty folders and measuring one measured the drive it sits on instead. Every drive
  picker in the app was offering seven drives that were not there. It now tests whether something
  is really mounted, the way the PS5 has since a backup was once copied onto a folder that only
  looked like a drive.

## Changed

- The front page is corrected in two places where it described an older design: the consoles *do*
  carry their own decompressor now and open a `.zip` in the same job that downloads it, and the
  entry for **Run** says what the console actually reports — the file it started and how big it
  was — rather than a check it did not make.
